# Floor-Plan-Diagram-for-TechSavvy-Inc.
Floor Plan Diagram for TechSavvy Inc.
Network Topology Design
The detail floor plan layout of TechSavvy company
	The detail of floor plan layouts are shown in figures, the details separation of departments and setting for each networking devices and network allocations can see in following figures.

a) Floor Plan Layout for TechSavvy Inc.
1.Explanation for First floor
	The first floor is also main entrance of the company there are also reception, public and cafeteria for guests. Workgroup switch_3 and 4 are installed in Customer Service and HR, Sale and Marketing departments that receive internet from two Multilayer core switches from the second floor. WG switch_3 distribute internet to Customer Service access point_7 for CS employees and Customer Service AP_9 for CS manager rooms and Public AP_8 for cafeteria and public library.
	WG switch-4 connected to HR AP_10 for members, HR Manager AP_11 its rooms and Sale and Marketing AP_12 for Sale and Marketing department that can also with different IP subnet for security reasons.
  
2.Explanation for second floor	
	The second floor perform as  the most important floor for network backbone of TechSavvy company because the main two internet cables enter from outside. The two cables of internet pass through to firewall_1 & 2 and flow into the Multilayer core switches_1 and 2 (performed as layer3) that are installed in server room.
  
  These multilayer core switches perform to allocate the internet to workgroup switches of the third floor, second floor and first floor. The workgroup switch_1 from IT support team support the internet to IT Support access point_1 for team members, Video Conferencing AP_2 for video conferencing room and IT Support AP_3 for the managers of IT support department and workgroup switch_2 for Software developer department flows internet to its Manager AP_4 for SD manager’s rooms, Software Development AP_5 for developers’ personal devices and Breakout AP_6 for breakout room and WC with different IP subnets.

3.Explanation for third floor	
	The management department and Finance department are placed on the third floor of the TechSavvy company according to the important role of the management and financial processfor company, not to be able to easily approach by strangers from outside. The Main Metting room on this floor is important for  internal and external meting for business matters. 	

The two workgroup switches that are connected to Multilayer switches for backbone internet from second floor are installed in each management department and finance department. 
	The internet from workgroup switch_5 flow to management manager access point_13 for CEO, managing directors and general manager room, management AP_14 for several management leaders and AP_15 for members of these department. Each access point is separated for network security reasons.
	The three access point for finance department that are connected from workgroup switch_6, Finance AP_16 is for the member of those department, Finance Manager AP_17 for managers and Breakout room AP_18 for dinning and WC of third floor. And Meeting Room AP_19 for main meeting room of third floor. Each AP is divided by different IP subnets.
